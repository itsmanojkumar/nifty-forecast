What this means

The forecast is the long-run upward trend carried forward. Past prices alone could not predict next month's move: Ridge and gradient boosting models scored about the same as guessing on direction. Treat the Dec 2027 number as a central scenario inside a wide range, and expect a typical error of roughly ±17%.

I also tested adding copper, aluminium, crude, gold, USD/INR, the dollar index, S&P 500, China and Nifty 50. None of them improved the forecast in a walk-forward test, so they are not used.
